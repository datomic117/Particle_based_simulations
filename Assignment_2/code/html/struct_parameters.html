◆ factor_12_nb

double factor_12_nb

Scaling factor for 1-2 (bonded) non-bonded interactions.

Scaling of the non-bonded interaction between atoms that are one, two or three bonds apart. Zero leaves the pair out of the non-bonded interaction altogether, one treats it like any other pair, and a value in between scales it, as several force fields do for 1-4 pairs.

A factor of one costs nothing: the connectivity is then never looked up, and the list of connections is not even built. That is what makes the non-bonded interaction independent of the connectivity, as it is in DPD.

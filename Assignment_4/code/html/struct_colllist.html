Detailed Description

The contacts between the particles and the walls.

Contacts between two particles are not listed here: they are the overlapping subset of the neighbor list, and the history they build up is kept beside that list in Nbrlist::tij. Only walls need a list of their own, since they are not part of the neighbor list.

◆ tiw

struct DeltaR* tiw

Tangential displacement of the contact between a particle and a wall, indexed as tiw[i*NUM_WALLS_MAX + w]. A particle and a wall are a fixed pair, so the history of their contact can simply stay where it is instead of being carried from one list of contacts to the next: it is set to zero for as long as the two are not touching, and builds up while they are.

Calculate all forces and torques on the particles: gravity, particle-particle contacts and particle-wall contacts.

The force of each particle is initialized to gravity and its torque to zero; the two contact routines then add their contributions and return their potential energy. Follow this convention when adding an interaction.

Parameters
p_parametersused members: g, A, freq, num_part, and the contact parameters
p_nbrlistused members: nbr, tij
p_colllistused members: num_contacts, contact, and the particle-wall contact data
[out]p_vectorsused members: r, v, omega, f, T
Returns
double potential energy of gravity-free conservative interactions (the contact springs)

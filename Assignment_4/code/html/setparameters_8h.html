◆ set_silo_geometry()

void set_silo_geometry ( struct Parameters *  p_parameters)

Sizes the silo and the box around it from the number of particles.

The radius of the silo follows from the number of particles and the aspect ratio H/R_cyl = 3 of the settled fill. The silo bottom plate sits at height z_silo, one fill height above the floor, so that the discharged material has room to fall and heap up below the orifice. The orifice diameter is D_ORIFICE mean particle diameters. For 7500 particles this gives R_cyl = 17.7 mm (a silo diameter of 17.7 d_p), z_silo = 53.1 mm and a box of 70.8 x 70.8 x 185.9 mm.

Parameters
[in,out]p_parametersmembers used: num_part, R_min, R_max; set: R_cyl, z_silo, R_orifice, orifice_open, L
